Bring the mod list closer to the original manager: drag rows to change the load order
(plus Top/Bottom and Alt+Home/End), sort by order, enabled state, title, pack, author,
update time or size without changing the launch order, an always-visible scrollbar,
mod thumbnails, author and "updated" columns, and a right-click menu with Workshop and
Steam links, Show in folder, copy path/name, categories, Keep always enabled and Hide.
Several mods can be selected with Ctrl/Shift+click or Ctrl+A and enabled, moved or hidden
together. Hidden mods have their own filter, categories can be edited and used as a filter, and
search accepts `/regex/`.
